    What CV measures

    Local stop

    Some lines run every 3 minutes, others every 10, so their gaps can't be compared as they stand. Stretch each line's clock until its average gap is the same length. What is left is unevenness, and the CV measures it.

    Ask the riders

    Express stop

    Riders land like darts thrown at the timeline, at moments that have nothing to do with the trains. That is the page's one assumption about riders. A long gap catches more darts than a short one, so ask each rider how long their gap was.

    The crowded car

    Local stop

    A train that ends a long gap picks up everyone who arrived during it. The average rider's train is fuller than the average train.

    Why trains bunch

    Local stop
